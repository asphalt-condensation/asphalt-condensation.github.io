## Verified product facts

- Offline, single-player psychological horror game.
- Directional audio is a core mechanic; headphones are recommended, not required.
- The player moves through five procedurally generated dungeon floors.
- Touch controls cover movement, temporary blackout, interactions/exits, and recovered memories.
- The app has no login, account, purchases, advertising, analytics, or network feature in the reviewed release source.
- The app stores selected language and a persistent in-game death count in `UserDefaults`.
- Deleting and reinstalling resets those local values.
