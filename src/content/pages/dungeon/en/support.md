Dungeon is an offline, landscape-only psychological horror game for iPhone and iPad.

> Support requests are handled through public GitHub Issues. You need a GitHub account to post. Do not include your real name, email address, device identifiers, screenshots containing personal information, or any other sensitive data.

## Recommended setup

- Use headphones for the intended directional-audio experience.
- Make sure the device volume is audible before starting a level.
- Rotate the device to either landscape orientation.
- For the most reliable audio, disconnect Bluetooth devices you are not using.

## Controls

- Hold and drag in the lower-left area to move.
- Tap the lower-right area to turn out the light temporarily.
- Use the hand button to open a chest or use an exit.
- Use the book button to review recovered memories.
- Tap menus and story overlays to continue.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot hear the game

Increase the system volume, disconnect unintended Bluetooth audio devices, and try headphones or the built-in speakers again. If the app was interrupted by a call or another audio app, return to Dungeon and resume play.

### The game does not rotate

Dungeon supports landscape orientation only. Disable the device's orientation lock, then rotate the device to either landscape direction.

### The controls do not respond as expected

Make sure you are touching the active control areas shown by the tutorial. Lift your finger before switching from movement to another action. If the problem continues, close and reopen the app.

### How do I reset local preferences?

Deleting and reinstalling Dungeon resets the selected language and locally stored death count. This cannot be undone.

## Before opening an issue

Please include the app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the shortest sequence that reproduces the problem. Search existing issues first so related reports can stay together.
